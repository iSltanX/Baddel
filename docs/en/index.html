Typed it in the wrong language? Switch it.

A small macOS menu bar tool that fixes text typed with the wrong keyboard layout between Arabic and English — with one shortcut, in any app, entirely on your Mac.

One shortcut fixes text typed with the wrong keyboard layout — in any app, entirely on your Mac.

Download for macOS

Baddel runs on a Mac — open this page on your computer.

How it works

1

Type as usual

Forgot to switch languages? Keep going — don’t delete.

2

Press ⌥⇧Space

Converts the selection or the last word; each press reaches one word further back.

3

Keep typing

The keyboard layout switches with it, and one Undo puts everything back.

Privacy isn’t a setting

Fully local

Every conversion happens on your Mac.

No account

No sign-up, no email.

No tracking

No analytics, no automatic reports.

One permission

Accessibility — never input monitoring.

Baddel never sends your text anywhere. A problem report goes out only after you’ve seen all of it and confirmed.

Install

1

Download and open the DMG

Drag Baddel to Applications.

2

Open it and grant permission

One switch in Accessibility.

3

If macOS warns

Open Anyway in Privacy & Security, once.
